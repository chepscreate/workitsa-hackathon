## 1. Pre-demo checklist (do the day before + 30 min before)
- [ ] **Two devices ready:** laptop = employer + admin views; phone = worker view (shows it's mobile-first). Same job visible on both is the "wow".
- [ ] **Employer account** signed in and approved (your own).
- [ ] **Worker account** signed in on the phone AND **approved + verified** (green Verified badge). Approve it yourself in `/admin` beforehand so verification shows as done.
- [ ] **2–3 open jobs already posted** in one suburb so `/find-work` isn't empty (gardening, cleaning, a trade — realistic titles + prices). Keep one you'll *hire on* live.
- [ ] **One completed job with a time-stamped "after" photo** already on the worker's public profile (`/u/...`) — this shows the new proof-of-work feature and a real rating.
- [ ] **Admin panel has real content** to show: verification queue, heatmap, audit log (it does — 118 users, 90 workers).
- [ ] **Browser tabs pre-opened** in order: workitsa.co.za (logged in) · `/post-a-job` · `/find-work` · a job in `/jobs` · `/u/<worker>` · `/admin`.
- [ ] **Deck + competitor PDF open** in the background as a fallback.
- [ ] **Screenshots of each key screen saved** on the desktop — if wifi drops or something errors, you keep moving. (Ask me to generate these.)
- [ ] **Charged phones, data on, notifications on** (so a push buzzes live during the demo — great moment).
- [ ] Do **one full dry-run** end to end the night before.

## 2. The walk-through (≈12–15 min) — what to click, what to say, what it answers

**Act 1 — 30-second frame (say, don't click).**
"Workit is one trusted marketplace for *any* everyday work — gardening to trades to
tutoring. Employers post, verified workers nearby offer, the job gets done, both
sides rate. Built trust-first, for prepaid Android. Let me show you it working."

**Act 2 — Trust & verification** *(answers Q1 signups, Q3 references)*
- Show a worker profile `/u/<worker>`: Verified badge, rating, completed-job count, **proof-of-work photos**.
- Open `/admin` → verification queue: "Every worker is ID-verified by a human — SA
  ID, selfie video, proof of address. This is the queue I review." Show one card.
- Line to land: *"Trust is earned from verified identity + rated jobs, not uploaded pics."*

**Act 3 — Employer posts a job** *(answers Q2 'live at launch', Q4)*
- On the laptop, `/post-a-job`: fill it in ~1 minute — title, category, price, suburb,
  optional photo, crew size. Show the **live preview**. Post it.
- Line: *"Under two minutes, no cost during launch."*

**Act 4 — Worker finds it & offers** *(matching)*
- On the phone, `/find-work`: the job you just posted appears, sorted by distance.
- Worker taps it → **accept the price or send a counter-offer** with a note. Send it.
- Back on laptop: the offer arrives (a **push notification** buzzes — call it out).

**Act 5 — Hire + safety + proof** *(answers Q3, Q8 disputes)*
- Employer accepts the offer → chat opens. Send a message + note **before/after photo sharing**.
- Show the safety stack on the job: **start-code** (read at the door), **live location**,
  **SOS to SAPS 10111**.
- Worker marks done → **"📷 Take a photo"** opens the camera; the saved photo is
  **time-stamped "Workit · date, time"**. *"This is the reference fix — evidence is
  captured live on the job, not uploaded. It's also the dispute evidence trail."*

**Act 6 — Complete, pay, rate** *(answers Q3 reputation)*
- Employer confirms, pays cash/EFT, **both sides rate**. Show the rating landing on the
  worker's profile. *"Reputation compounds from real jobs."*

**Act 7 — Differentiator: crew jobs** *(answers Q6)*
- Briefly show a crew job ("1 of 3 hired"). *"Hire a whole team in one post — moving
  day, events. No competitor does this."*

**Act 8 — Admin cockpit** *(answers Q7 moderation, Q8 disputes)*
- `/admin`: overview KPIs + trends, moderation queue (auto-flags + reports),
  heatmap, disputes (freeze + evidence review), audit log.
- Line: *"Automation-first moderation — cheap to run, scales without a big manual team."*

**Act 9 — Close (deck, 1 min).** Monetization (free now → service fee → R25/R50
subscription), go-to-market (density-first, B2B wedge), and the ask.

## 3. Talking points for the hard questions
- **"How many jobs / how much traction?"** Be straight: *"We deliberately built the
  full platform and the supply side first — 90 workers in with almost no spend. Demand
  activation is the current phase, and it's exactly where this round/partnership goes."*
  (Don't overstate; the pilot did 7 jobs and proved the loop.)
- **"Customer acquisition / breakeven (Q9)?"** Density-first, low-CAC (WhatsApp +
  referrals + ambassadors), **B2B/SME recurring demand**, and a **radically lower cost
  base** (founder-built) so breakeven needs far less volume than SweepSouth/Kandua.
- **Harambee angle:** *"Your SA Youth network could be our supply pipeline; our
  outcomes data (jobs done, rand earned) is what your programmes report on — a natural
  delivery/outcomes partnership."*
