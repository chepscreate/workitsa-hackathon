**Goal:** SELL the app's offering to the panel in a tiny "traffic-light" window.

## THE SCRIPT (record this)

> "Nearly half of South Africa's youth can't find work — not for lack of skill,
> but lack of access. **WorkitSA turns WhatsApp into a job.** No app. No data.
> Register, get matched, get hired, and walk away with **proof of work** — all in
> a chat you already have. It's **live today.** Workit — everyday work, found
> nearby."

~48 words · lands at ~20s at a brisk-but-clear pace.

### Beat map (so you know where to breathe)
1. **Hook (0–5s):** "Nearly half of SA's youth can't find work — not for lack of skill, but lack of access." → say it straight to camera, serious.
2. **The offer (5–14s):** "WorkitSA turns WhatsApp into a job. No app. No data. Register, get matched, get hired, and walk away with proof of work — all in a chat you already have." → lift energy, confident.
3. **Close (14–20s):** "It's live today. Workit — everyday work, found nearby." → slow down, land the tagline.

## Delivery notes
- **To camera**, phone held steady (or propped) at eye level, vertical is fine for the short video.
- Good light on your face; quiet room; one clean take beats a polished mess.
- **"No app. No data."** — hit those two as short, punchy beats (the differentiator).
- Smile on the close; sound like a founder who already has a live product (you do).

## Optional on-screen (if you want to cut in a shot or two)
- A 1–2s clip of the live WhatsApp chat (the onboarding or the "You're hired" message).
- The homepage at workitsa.co.za.
- Keep it minimal — a 20s pitch is mostly YOU selling, not a demo.
