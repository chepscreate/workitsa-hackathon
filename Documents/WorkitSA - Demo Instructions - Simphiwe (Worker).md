# Demo Recording — Simphiwe (Worker side, WhatsApp)

You are screen-recording the **worker experience** on your phone via WhatsApp. You play a worker called "Sipho" who finds and accepts a job without downloading any app.

## Before you start
- Open WhatsApp on your phone
- Find the chat with **+27 74 320 7131** (WorkitSA business number, has a W logo)
- Turn on your phone's screen recorder
- Wait for Tshepo to WhatsApp you **"GO"**

## Your steps

### Step 1 — Text JOBS
- When Tshepo says GO, type **JOBS** and send it to the WorkitSA number
- You'll get a list of open jobs nearby
- **Wait a moment** — Tshepo just posted a new job ("Garden clean-up, R350, Soweto")
- It should appear in the list

### Step 2 — Make an offer
- Reply with the **number** of the Garden clean-up job (e.g. **1**)
- OR reply with a number and your own price to counter-offer (e.g. **1 400**)
- You'll get a confirmation that your offer was sent

### Step 3 — Get hired
- Wait for Tshepo to accept your offer on his side
- You should receive a message saying **"You're hired!"** or similar
- Make sure your screen recorder captures this moment — it's the money shot

## Tips
- Keep your phone in **portrait mode**
- Make sure WhatsApp notifications are ON so the messages pop up visually
- Don't rush — let each message arrive and sit on screen for 2-3 seconds before the next action
- If something goes wrong, just text **JOBS** again to restart

## That's it!
Send your screen recording to Tshepo when done. He'll stitch everything together in CapCut.
