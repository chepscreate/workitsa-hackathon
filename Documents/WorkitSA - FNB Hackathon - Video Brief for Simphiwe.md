## What is this, in one minute?

- **The hackathon:** FNB (the bank) runs an "App of the Year" competition. We're
  entering **WorkitSA** under the *"Youth employment & work readiness"* challenge.
- **The video is a required part of our entry.** Judges watch a **90-second demo
  video** to see the app in action and score us on it — so a clear, confident video
  genuinely helps us win.
- **Our angle:** WorkitSA connects young South Africans to everyday work nearby. For
  the hackathon we built a **WhatsApp Job Channel** — someone with *no data and no
  app* can find work, do it, and walk away with a **shareable Work Record** (a proof
  of work), all inside one WhatsApp chat. That whole journey is the star of the video.

## The golden rules (please stick to these)

1. **Maximum 90 seconds.** Hard limit — if it's 91 seconds it can be disqualified.
   Aim for ~85.
2. **Speak slowly and clearly.** The script is short *on purpose* so you never rush.
3. **Add captions/subtitles** (text on screen) — many people watch on mute.
4. **Film on a phone is perfectly fine.** Hold it steady or prop it up.
5. **Quiet room** for the voice. Recording your voice separately and talking over the
   screen recording is best — but one clean take is okay too.

## The script — 6 short beats (this is the whole video)

Say the line in **"quotes"**, film what's in *italics*. Times are a guide.

*Film: the WorkitSA logo / home page.*

**2. What WorkitSA is (0:10–0:20)**
*Film: scroll the "Find work" job list at workitsa.co.za.*

**6. Close (1:12–1:30)**

## Exact taps for the WhatsApp parts (beats 3–5)

In the **"WorkitSA Jobs"** chat, one message at a time, waiting for each reply:

1. Send **`JOBS`** → the numbered job list comes back.
2. Send **`1`** → "✅ Offer sent".
3. *(Tshepo accepts your offer now — give him a moment.)*
4. Send **`START`** → "👍 Marked … as started".
5. Send **`DONE`** → "🎉 Marked … as done".
6. The **"🎉 … Here's your Work Record …"** message arrives → **tap the link** →
   let the page load, scroll slowly once.
