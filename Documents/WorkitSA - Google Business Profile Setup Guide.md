# WorkitSA — Google Business Profile setup guide

A free Google Business Profile (GBP) puts WorkitSA on **Google Search and Maps** with
your logo, link, contact, photos and reviews. It's the single best *free* way to show
up on Google. This guide walks you through it start to finish — no tech needed.

---

## ⚠ Read this first — one eligibility rule
Google Business Profile is meant for businesses that make **in-person contact with
customers** (either a shopfront people visit, or a **service-area business** that goes
out to customers). A purely online-only app can be refused or later **suspended**.

**How to stay on the right side of it:** list the *company behind the app* as a
**service-area business** serving Gauteng — i.e. **Futhersky Solutions, trading as
WorkitSA**, based in Alberton, connecting people with workers across Gauteng. That's a
legitimate local-service positioning (you do operate locally and meet workers/clients).
Keep all details truthful and consistent with your CIPC registration, and never post
fake reviews — that's what triggers suspensions.

If Google ever rejects it as "online only," that's okay — the free organic/SEO +
Google Search Console route still works, and we can lean on those instead.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Open Google Business Profile
Go to **google.com/business** → click **Manage now** → sign in with the Google account
you chose above.

### 2. Business name
Type **WorkitSA**. If it already exists, claim it; otherwise click **"Create a business
with this name."**

### 3. Business category
- **Primary category:** `Software company` (most accurate for the app).
- **Add secondary categories** later for discovery: `Employment agency`,
  `Temp agency`, or `Internet marketing service`. These help people searching for
  work/staff find you.

### 4. Location — choose service-area (keep your address private)
- When asked *"Do you have a location customers can visit?"* → choose **No**.
- This makes it a **service-area business**: your home/Alberton address stays **hidden**,
  and you instead list the **areas you serve**.
- **Add service areas:** Alberton, Johannesburg, Pretoria, Benoni, Roodepoort,
  Kempton Park, Soweto, Sandton, Germiston — i.e. greater **Gauteng**.

### 5. Contact details
- **Phone:** your chosen public number.
- **Website:** `https://workitsa.co.za`

### 6. Finish & verify
- Click through to finish. Google will ask you to **verify** you own the business —
  usually by **video verification** (a short video of you/the business on your phone,
  following their prompts) for service-area businesses, or sometimes by phone/email.
- Follow the on-screen steps. Verification can take anywhere from minutes to a few days.

## After it's approved — fill it out fully (this is what gets you found)
1. **Description** (paste this, tweak as you like):
   > WorkitSA is a South African marketplace for everyday work. People and businesses
   > post jobs — gardening, cleaning, trades, moving, deliveries, errands — and nearby
   > ID-verified workers accept or quote, do the job, and get paid. Free during launch
   > in Gauteng. Built for low-data phones, in English, Afrikaans and isiZulu.
2. **Logo + photos:** upload your logo and 3–5 images (app screenshots, a tidy-garden
   "after" photo, etc.). Listings with photos get far more clicks.
3. **Services:** add each work category (Gardening, Cleaning, Plumbing, Moving,
   Tutoring, Deliveries…) as a service — great for search matching.
4. **Hours:** set them (e.g. Mon–Sat 08:00–18:00, or "Open 24 hours" since it's an app).
5. **Website button + "Get quote" / message:** point to https://workitsa.co.za.
6. **Google Posts:** you can post updates just like Facebook — reuse the captions from
   `marketing/queue/fb-2026-10.json`.
7. **Reviews:** once you have a few happy employers/workers, ask them to leave a Google
   review (share your profile's review link). Real reviews boost ranking a lot — never
   use fake ones.
