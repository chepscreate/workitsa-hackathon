# WorkitSA — Demo Voiceover Script (Split-Screen)

**Format:** Two phones/screens side by side — LEFT = Employer (posting the job on web), RIGHT = Worker (accepting via WhatsApp).

---

## 0–8s · Opening — Homepage
**Left:** Homepage with map and job pins
**Right:** Worker's WhatsApp (idle, waiting)

> "In South Africa, millions of people are ready to work — they just can't find it. Workit connects them, instantly."

---

## 8–20s · Employer Posts a Job
**Left:** Employer logs in → fills in job details → hits Post
**Right:** Still idle — waiting for the alert

> "An employer posts a job — a garden clean-up in Soweto for three-fifty. It takes seconds."

---

## 20–30s · Worker Gets the Alert
**Left:** Job posted, employer waits
**Right:** WhatsApp notification pops in — "New job near you"

> "And just like that — a nearby worker gets it on WhatsApp. No app download. No data needed."

---

## 30–45s · Worker Views Details and Accepts
**Left:** Employer waiting for offers
**Right:** Worker replies VIEW → sees full job details → replies ACCEPT

> "She views the details — the job, the pay, the location — and accepts, all without leaving WhatsApp."

---

## 45–58s · Employer Hires
**Left:** Offer appears → employer clicks Accept/Hire
**Right:** Worker receives "You're hired" confirmation

> "The employer sees the offer and hires — the worker is notified instantly. That's it. Connected."

---

## 58–70s · Safety and Proof
**Left:** Start-code screen / Work Record
**Right:** Worker's WhatsApp showing job confirmed

> "Every job comes with a start code, an SOS button, and a digital Work Record — proof of real work done."

---

## 70–80s · Closing
**Left:** Admin panel — Live Pulse / Jarvis flash
**Right:** Fade or hold on "You're hired" message

> "Behind it all, an AI manages the marketplace — matching, verifying, keeping it safe. This is Workit. Live today at workitsa.co.za."

---

**Total: ~80 seconds**

## Recording tips
- Speak at a calm, confident pace — not rushed
- Pause naturally between sections (the editor can tighten gaps)
- If recording voiceover separately from video, clap or say "mark" at the start so the editor can sync
- Keep energy warm but grounded — this is about real impact, not hype
