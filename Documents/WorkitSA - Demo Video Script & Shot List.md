# WorkitSA — Demo Video (≤90s) · Shot list + timed captions

**Setup:** Employer = web (judge employer login, workitsa.co.za). Worker = WhatsApp on **081 (Sipho)**.
**Clean-post recipe:** employer posts a **home-garden / gardening** job in **Johannesburg or Soweto** → only Sipho gets alerted (no real-user spam).
Record each segment separately, stitch in CapCut, burn in the captions below (judges watch muted).

| Time | Shot | On-screen caption |
|------|------|-------------------|
| 0–8s | Homepage at workitsa.co.za (real jobs on the map) | **WorkitSA — everyday work, found nearby.** Live today. |
| 8–20s | Judge employer logs in (web) → posts "Garden clean-up · R350 · Soweto" | **An employer posts a job in seconds.** |
| 20–40s ⭐ | Sipho's phone: WhatsApp alert arrives → text **JOBS** → list shows → reply / **OFFER** | **The worker gets it on WhatsApp — no app, no data.** Text JOBS → apply. |
| 40–55s ⭐ | Employer (web) sees the offer → clicks Accept/Hire → Sipho's phone shows **"✅ You're hired"** | **The employer hires — the worker is notified instantly.** |
| 55–68s | Start-code / Work Record (web or screenshot) | **Safe by design: start-code, SOS, and a Work Record as proof.** |
| 68–75s | Quick flash of Jarvis / Live Pulse in /admin → end card | **An AI that runs the marketplace. Try it: workitsa.co.za** |

⭐ = protect these segments if you run short on time — the WhatsApp parts are what win points.

## Tips
- Have Sipho **text first** (JOBS) so he's "in-window" — his alert then arrives as a rich, full-detail message (nicer than the template).
- Portrait is fine (lots of it is phone WhatsApp). Keep the file **< 30MB**.
- Upload **unlisted to YouTube** or Google Drive → paste the link into deck Slides 8 & 12.
