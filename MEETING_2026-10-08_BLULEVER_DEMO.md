# BluLever meeting — demo runbook (Thu 8 Oct 2026, 10:00 SAST, Google Meet)

**Goal of the demo:** show that Workit is where a BluLever graduate *earns, proves good work,
and gets a verified reputation* — and that it produces the outcome data BluLever's funders/ISAs need.
Frame everything as **"imagine this worker is your BluLever plumbing/electrical graduate."**

## Pre-flight (5 min before)
- Sign in to **https://workitsa.co.za** as admin (your account) on a stable connection.
- Open two tabs: **(1)** the job feed / a worker profile, **(2)** **/admin/live** (Live Pulse).
- Confirm the **Alberton demo data** is showing (5 jobs incl. a **plumbing** job). ✅ verified live 8 Oct:
  employers (Nomvula Dlamini, Aisha Patel, Bongani Zulu, Karabo Mahlangu, Pieter van der Merwe),
  workers (Sipho Nkosi, Thandi Mokoena, Lerato Dube, Johan Botha), jobs incl.
  **"Fix a leaking kitchen tap" (plumbing, open)** and **"Garden clean-up & lawn mow" (completed)**.
- Have the **one-pager** open to send in chat at the end: `Documents/Workit x BluLever - Partnership One-Pager.pdf`.

## 3-minute flow (hero the plumbing job)
1. **Trust first (~30s)** — Open a verified worker profile (e.g. Sipho Nkosi). Show ID-verified badge,
   skills, ratings, completed jobs. *"This is exactly where a 'BluLever-verified' badge sits — an employer
   sees proven skill, not a CV claim. That's your brand as the quality mark."*
2. **Demand → match (~45s)** — Open **"Fix a leaking kitchen tap"** (plumbing, Alberton). Show post →
   nearby verified workers respond / counter-offer → employer picks. *"A BluLever plumbing grad gets a
   real, paying job in their own area."*
3. **Proof + reputation (~45s)** — Open the **completed** "Garden clean-up & lawn mow" job → show the
   **time-stamped proof-of-work photo** + two-way rating. *"This is the outcome evidence your impact
   funders, B-BBEE sponsors and ISAs depend on — verified earning and good work, captured automatically."*
4. **The god-view (~30s, the wow)** — Open **/admin/live**, hit **Simulate** → Alberton heatmap, workers /
   available-now / offers / jobs, hire arcs. *"This is the local marketplace we're densifying — Alberton
   first. Imagine BluLever grads lighting up this map."*
5. **Close (~30s)** — *"So: your grads earn, you get the outcome data, and a BluLever badge becomes the
   quality mark. Shall we run a small pilot?"* → hand over the one-pager.

## Fallbacks / don't-fumble
- If the **live** feed looks thin, use **Simulate** on /admin/live (it's Alberton-clustered) for the visual.
- Demo jobs/workers are visible once **signed in** — have your admin login ready; don't fumble sign-in on screen.
- **Don't over-claim volume.** Lead with the *infrastructure* (verification, proof-of-work, the worker record)
  and the *pilot*, not big numbers. Workit is early — that's fine; the pilot is the point.

## Talking points (the strategic frame)
- BluLever earns on **outcomes + graduate earning** (ISAs repay when grads earn; impact/B-BBEE/SETA sponsors
  need proof of jobs). So position Workit as **"we make your outcomes real and measurable,"** not "send us your grads."
- Workit = **complement**, not replacement, for formal employer placements — it's the extra earning +
  entrepreneurial/self-employment channel (matches their "self-leading artisan" language).

## Ask them (let them talk first)
- How do grads / self-leading artisans find work *between and after* placements — where's the gap?
- What do your 150+ artisan-SMEs struggle with: demand, crew, admin?
- What outcome data do you report to funders/SETAs — would verified earning/work-history help?
- Would a "BluLever-verified" badge on a third-party platform fit your brand & quality bar?
- Which trade + area would you pilot first?
