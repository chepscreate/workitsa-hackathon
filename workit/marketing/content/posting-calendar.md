# Workit — 2-week launch posting calendar (starter)

A light, repeatable rhythm for the pre-launch / launch push in Gauteng. ~1 post a day
across the channels you have. Adjust to the accounts you actually run. The goal is
**demand-led**: roughly alternate worker-side and employer/business-side so both sides
of the marketplace grow together (see the admin Growth dashboard).

Legend: 🟠 worker-side · 🟢 employer/household · 🔵 business · ⚪ trust/brand

## Cadence tips
- **Best local times:** ~07:00 (commute), ~12:30 (lunch), ~19:00 (evening). WhatsApp
  status does well early morning + evening.
- **Repurpose, don’t reinvent:** one poster → FB post + WA status + IG. One video → reels
  on IG + TikTok + FB.
- **Always end with the CTA + link** (workitsa.co.za) and the QR where the format allows.
- **Pin the survey** in week 2 to gather what people actually need — feeds the price
  bands and the roadmap.
