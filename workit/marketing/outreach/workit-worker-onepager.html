Workit For workers · Gauteng launch

Get paid for the skills you already have.

Workit is a South African app where people near you post everyday jobs — gardening, cleaning, plumbing, hair, moving, deliveries and more. You see the job and the price, accept it or name your own price, do the work, and get paid cash or EFT the same day.

Never pay to apply. Workit is free for workers. No joining fee, no "admin fee", no airtime to apply. Anyone who asks you for money to get a job is a scammer — on Workit, money only moves one way: to you.

Jobs near you

See open jobs sorted by distance from home. Works on any phone, in English, Afrikaans or isiZulu, and easy on data.

You set your price

Accept the employer's price or send a counter-offer. Nothing starts until you've both agreed.

Verified means trusted

A real person checks your ID — then employers see your ✓ Verified badge and hire with confidence. Employers can even search for your exact skill and invite you directly.

Safety built in

Live tracking you can share with family, a one-tap SOS button on every active job, and two-way ratings so bad actors don't last.
