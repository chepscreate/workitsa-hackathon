# Worker recruitment — job-seeker groups & communities to reach out to

Researched 2026-07-06 (web). Goal: fill the **supply side** (verified workers)
in Gauteng by meeting unemployed job seekers where they already gather.
Complements `strategy.md` (which targets the demand side / businesses).

**Ground rules (same as the rest of this kit):** everything here is
draft-and-review — nothing gets posted, joined, or emailed without the
founder's say-so. Respect each group's rules (many ban ads — message the
**admin first**, don't spam members). POPIA: we contact organisations and
public admins, never scrape member lists. This audience is heavily targeted
by job scams (fake "pay-to-apply" schemes), so every message must lead with
what makes Workit safe: **free to join, verified by a real person, never pay
to apply, cash/EFT on completion**.

## Tier 1 — institutional partners (biggest reach, most legitimacy)

These exist precisely to connect unemployed people to opportunities. Workit
can plug in as an "opportunity holder" — often for free.

| Who | What | Why it fits | First move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SA Youth / Harambee** (sayouth.mobi) | National network, 3.9M+ young job seekers (18–35), **zero-rated** (no data cost — same low-data ethos as Workit). Free for opportunity holders to list. | Exactly our worker demographic; zero-rated matters to our users. | Register Workit as an **opportunity holder** on SA Youth; ask Harambee about listing gig/earning opportunities (they explicitly support "opportunities outside the formal sector"). |
| **Nasi iSpani** (Gauteng provincial programme) | Premier's flagship: 45,218 training opportunities relaunched Sept 2025 — training **bricklaying, plumbing, electrical, painting, welding, tiling, paving, landscaping, auto repairs**. | Their graduates are trained in *exactly* Workit's trade categories and need somewhere to earn. Gauteng-only, like our launch. | Contact the programme office: pitch Workit as the "what's next" for graduates — a free platform to earn from their new trade. Hotline 0800 22 88 27. |
| **NYDA** (National Youth Development Agency) | Walk-in branches + district offices across Gauteng; life-skills + job-prep programmes. | Branch noticeboards + programme cohorts = physical distribution for the QR posters already in `content/`. | Visit/write to the nearest NYDA branch; ask to display the "Find work" poster + present Workit to a cohort. |
| **Dept of Employment & Labour — labour centres (ESSA)** | Government employment services; unemployed people register in person. | Same poster/noticeboard play, official credibility. | Enquire at a Gauteng labour centre about registering Workit as a private employment platform / displaying materials. |

## Tier 2 — Gauteng NGOs doing skills & work-readiness

Their graduates finish a course and immediately need income — a warm,
trusted channel if the NGO endorses us.

- **Afrika Tikkun** (afrikatikkun.org) — cradle-to-career development incl.
  skills + placement, strong JHB township presence (incl. Braamfontein, Alex,
  Diepsloot). Ask about presenting to their career-development cohorts.
- **Khulisa Social Solutions** (khulisa.org.za) — community empowerment +
  youth development across SA, JHB-rooted.
- **SOS Children's Villages SA — Youth Employability programme** — prepares
  youth for the labour market; small but very warm referrals.
- *(For later provinces: Amy Foundation & Sozo Foundation — both Cape Town —
  same pitch when Workit expands to the Western Cape.)*

## Tier 4 — WhatsApp & Telegram job groups

The Citizen (2024) estimates **~850 WhatsApp job groups nationally**, each
with thousands of members, run by volunteer admins who vet posts for scams
(example: Karen Lloyd, JHB + surrounds, 5,000+ members across her groups).
Admins are gatekeepers AND allies — they already fight the scams Workit's
verification solves.

- Telegram (sizes verified 2026-07-06 via t.me previews): **@jobcareafrica**
  (2,483 subs — the only one worth contacting), **@RSA_Opportunities** (525
  subs, but also runs a WhatsApp group + channel and usefuldata.info — small
  multi-platform operator, may punch above its weight). ~~@jobs27southafrica~~
  (231 subs, posts mostly sport — dropped), ~~@southafricavacancy~~ (133
  subs — dropped). **Fursa Opportunities** (WhatsApp channel +
  fursaopportunities.com) unverified size.
- Lesson from verification: Telegram is thin for SA job seekers — the real
  volume is Facebook groups + WhatsApp groups (sizes only visible once
  joined). Weight effort accordingly.
- Approach: ask admins to share a Workit "verified gig work near you — free,
  never pay to apply" card with the QR code. Offer them a referral angle:
  the in-app referral code system (Connector/Ambassador badges) lets an
  admin measure the sign-ups they drive.

## Tier 5 — "CareerTok" creators (TikTok / X / Instagram)

IOL (June 2026) covers SA's CareerTok wave — creators with big followings of
job-hunting youth. Best treated as micro-influencer outreach: a short brief +
demo account, let them make the content.

- **JOBS UPDATE WITH OchyBerry** — multi-platform (WhatsApp, Telegram, FB,
  TikTok, X, IG); shares vacancies + links.
- **TJRemoteCareers** — big but remote-work-focused; weaker fit, low priority.
- Find 3–5 more via TikTok search "jobs South Africa" / "vacancies Johannesburg"
  and rank by Gauteng audience + engagement (do this in-app; search engines
  can't see follower counts reliably).
