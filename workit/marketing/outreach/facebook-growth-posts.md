# WorkitSA — Facebook growth post bank

Your proven channel: posting in Facebook unemployment/jobs groups drove all the
worker sign-ups. This is a rotating bank so you never post the same text twice
(Facebook flags identical repeated posts as spam and can restrict the account).

## Rules that keep the channel alive
- **Rotate the copy.** Never paste the identical message across many groups — pick
  a different variant each time; tweak a word or two.
- **Space it out.** 2–4 groups a day, not 30 in one hour. Slow and steady beats a
  ban.
- **Post from your normal profile**, and reply to comments (engagement stops posts
  looking like spam and boosts reach).
- **Pin the link once** and let people ask questions in comments.
- **Track what works** — note which groups actually produce sign-ups; double down
  on those, drop the dead ones.
- **Keep the `?src=fb` tag on the link.** Every link below ends in
  `https://workitsa.co.za/?src=fb`. The site captures that tag on first visit and
  stamps `signup_source = fb` on any account that person creates — so you can count
  Facebook sign-ups in the **Admin → Users CSV export** (`signup_source` column).
  Don't strip the tag. For other channels use a different tag, e.g. radio →
  `?src=radio`, WhatsApp → `?src=wa`.
