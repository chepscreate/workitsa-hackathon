**From:** Tshepo M, Founder — Workit (Furthersky Solutions)
**Email:** tshepowayne@workitsa.co.za · **Web:** workitsa.co.za

Dear [Name / Team],

[RECIPIENT-SPECIFIC OPENING — see variants below]

Workit (workitsa.co.za) is a South African gig-work marketplace, live now
and launching province by province, starting with Gauteng. Local households
and businesses post everyday jobs — gardening, cleaning, skilled trades,
beauty, moving, deliveries, childcare, tutoring — and nearby workers accept
the offered price or negotiate their own. Payment is cash or EFT on
completion of the work.

We built Workit around three principles that I believe align with your
mission:

1. **Trust and safety first.** Every worker's SA ID and selfie video are
   reviewed by a real person before they can take a job. Workers never pay
   anything — no joining fees, no fees to apply — which directly counters
   the recruitment scams that prey on job seekers.
2. **Built for real conditions.** The app runs on low-end Android phones,
   is light on data, and works in English, Afrikaans and isiZulu.
3. **Dignity through earning.** Workers set their own prices, build a
   public track record through ratings and completed jobs, and earn
   reputation badges that make each next job easier to win.

I am writing to ask whether we might work together to connect the people
you serve with earning opportunities on Workit. Concretely, that could be
as simple as:

- sharing Workit with your [graduates / members / registered work-seekers]
  via your existing channels (we can supply a one-page flyer with a QR
  code, print-ready);
- allowing us to present Workit briefly to a cohort or at a centre; or
- listing Workit as an earning opportunity on your platform, where
  applicable.

There is no cost to your organisation or to the workers — Workit is free
for workers, permanently, and free for employers during our launch. We are
a small, founder-run South African company, and I would welcome the chance
to demonstrate the platform to your team — in person in Gauteng or on a
call, at your convenience.

Thank you for the work you do. I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,

**Tshepo M**
