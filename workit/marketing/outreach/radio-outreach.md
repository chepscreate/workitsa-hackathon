# WorkitSA — Radio outreach playbook

Radio is a strong fit: it reaches work-seekers who are low on data, and a young
founder fighting unemployment is an easy "yes" for talk, youth and community shows.

## Three ways in (use all three)
1. **Be a guest (best).** Pitch a 5–10 min on-air chat about youth unemployment and
   a homegrown solution. Talk/youth/community shows love this. Send to the station's
   general/guest email or DM the show's producer.
2. **Community Service Announcement (CSA) — FREE.** Community stations air short
   announcements for community-benefit initiatives. A free platform helping the
   unemployed qualifies. Ask them to run the 20-second script below.
3. **News tip.** For a news slot — send to the newsroom/general contact.

## Best-fit stations (Gauteng / East Rand first)

### Community radio — highest fit for work-seekers, easiest access, free CSAs
Community stations are the most reachable (often via Facebook/WhatsApp) and cover
your Alberton/East Rand beachhead. Search Facebook for these + "community radio",
and message their page or find the contact on their FB "About":

- Alex FM (Alexandra), Eldos FM, Westside FM, Kasie FM, Thetha FM, Jozi FM (Soweto)

make a real dent in youth unemployment, and it's already working.
