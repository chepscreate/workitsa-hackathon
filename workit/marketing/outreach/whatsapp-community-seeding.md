# WhatsApp community seeding — groups, churches, stokvels

Drafted 2026-07-08. Companion to `admin-outreach-message.md` (which covers
Facebook/Telegram group admins). This file is for **WhatsApp** community reach,
where trust runs through a **person** — a group admin, a pastor, a stokvel
chairlady — not a feed.

> **Draft-and-review: the founder sends these personally.** Nothing automated,
> nothing batched. WhatsApp especially — mass-messaging strangers gets your
> number banned and burns trust. One conversation at a time.

## The non-negotiables (every message, every channel)

- **Leader-first.** Message the admin / pastor / organiser privately. NEVER
  drop a promo into a community group uninvited — that's exactly the spam
  those groups fight.
- **Lead with the three things** that separate Workit from a scam, because
  that's the first thing a careful community leader will wonder about you:
  **free to join · every worker ID-verified by a real person · you NEVER pay
  to apply.**
- **You're a person, not a brand.** Tshepo, a Gauteng founder. Say so.
- **Give an easy out.** "If it's not a fit, no problem at all." A leader's
  standing with their people is the asset you're borrowing — never strain it.
- **One follow-up maximum**, 4–6 days later. No reply after that = leave it.
- **Personalise the [brackets].** People smell copy-paste in seconds.
- **Never ask for or offer money.** Not for members, not to the leader.
- A warm SA greeting opens doors: **Sawubona / Dumela / Lotjhani / Goeie dag** —
  match the community.

## 1. Community WhatsApp group (message the ADMIN)

**First message:**

> Sawubona [name] 👋 My name is Tshepo — I'm a South African founder, born and
> raised here in Gauteng. I built a free app called Workit (workitsa.co.za):
> people nearby post everyday jobs — gardening, cleaning, plumbing, hair,
> bakkie trips, deliveries — and workers get paid cash or EFT when the job's
> done.
>
> I know [group name] works hard to keep scams out, so I want to be upfront:
> Workit is **free for workers, nobody ever pays to apply**, and every worker
> is **ID-verified by a real person** before they can take a job. That's the
> whole reason I built it this way.
>
> Would you be open to me sharing it with your members? I can send you a
> one-page flyer and a link to look over first — no pressure at all, and if
> it's not right for your group I completely understand. Either way, thank you
> for what you do for people looking for work. 🙏

**Follow-up (once, 4–6 days later):**

> Hi [name], just floating this up in case it got buried — no rush. Quick
> recap: free for your members, never pay to apply, every worker ID-checked by
> a real person, works on any phone and light on data, in English, Afrikaans
> and isiZulu. Happy to answer anything — and if it's not a fit for [group
> name], no problem at all. 🙏

**If the admin says yes — give them THIS to share** (invite them to reword it
into the group's voice; a message in the admin's own tone lands better):

> 🟢 *Find paid work near you — free (Gauteng)*
>
> A neighbour posts a job — garden, cleaning, a leaking tap, a bakkie trip —
> you see the job *and* the price. Accept it, or name your own price. Get paid
> cash or EFT the same day it's done.
>
> ✅ 100% free — you NEVER pay to apply (anyone asking for money is a scam)
> ✅ Every worker is ID-verified by a real person, so employers trust you
> ✅ Any phone, light on data — English / Afrikaans / isiZulu
> ✅ Safety built in: share your live location with family + an SOS button
>
> Join free 👉 workitsa.co.za/signup

## 2. Church (message the PASTOR / church office / ministry admin)

Churches are trust hubs — but approach them as a **service to the
congregation**, never a sales pitch. Frame it around dignity and protecting
members from work-seeker scams.

**First message:**

> Good day Pastor [surname], my name is Tshepo M — a Gauteng-based founder and
> [if true: a member of the church community / a fellow believer]. I hope
> you're well.
>
> Many people in our communities are looking for honest work, and sadly the
> "pay us a fee and we'll find you a job" scams prey on exactly those people. I
> built a free app, Workit (workitsa.co.za), to offer a safer path: locals
> post everyday jobs — gardening, cleaning, trades, childcare, deliveries —
> and members of the congregation could find paid work near home, **completely
> free, never paying a cent to join or apply**, with every worker ID-verified
> by a real person.
>
> Would the church consider letting me share this with members who are seeking
> work — perhaps a notice on the board, a line in the bulletin, or a brief
> word after a service? I'd gladly bring printed flyers and explain it to your
> team first. There's no cost to the church or to anyone, ever.
>
> Thank you for the work you do for the community. God bless. 🙏

**Ready-to-read announcement / bulletin line** (give this to the church office):

> *A word for anyone seeking work:* Workit is a free South African app where
> you can find paid jobs near you — gardening, cleaning, trades, and more. It
> is **free to join, you never pay to apply**, and every worker is ID-checked
> by a real person. Sign up free at **workitsa.co.za/signup**, or speak to
> [contact] for a flyer.

## 3. Stokvel (message the CHAIRLADY / secretary / treasurer)

Stokvels are dense trust networks — perfect for word-of-mouth, and members are
often exactly the people who'd value **extra income between contributions**.
The "no fees, ever" point matters doubly here, since the group already pools
real money.

**First message:**

> Dumela [name] 🙏 I'm Tshepo, a Gauteng founder. I understand you run/help lead
> [stokvel name] — I have huge respect for how stokvels help members build
> something together.
>
> I built a free app, Workit (workitsa.co.za), that might help your members
> earn a little extra between contributions: people nearby post everyday jobs
> (cleaning, gardening, cooking for events, hair, bakkie trips) and members
> could take the ones that suit them and get paid cash or EFT on the day.
>
> Two things I want to be clear about upfront, because I know the group handles
> real money: Workit is **100% free — no joining fee, and you never pay to
> apply for a job** (anyone asking for money is a scam), and every worker is
> **ID-verified by a real person**.
>
> Could I share a short flyer and link with you to pass on to members, if you
> think it could help them? No pressure at all — and thank you for what you do
> for your group. 🙏

**Ready-to-share member message** (for the chairlady to forward in the group):

> 💚 *Extra earning for members — free*
>
> Workit is a free app where you can pick up paid jobs near home — cleaning,
> gardening, cooking for functions, hair, deliveries — and get paid cash or
> EFT the same day. **Free to join, you never pay to apply**, every worker
> ID-verified. Great for a little extra between our contributions.
>
> Join free 👉 workitsa.co.za/signup

## 4. Your own WhatsApp Status (allowed — it's your contacts, not a group)

Post to your Status every few days (people scroll it like a feed). Rotate:

> 🟢 Looking for work in Gauteng — or need a hand at home? Workit connects you
> with verified workers nearby. Free to join, you never pay to apply.
> workitsa.co.za 💪

> 🛠️ Whatever the job — gardening, cleaning, a leaking tap — someone nearby on
> Workit can do it. Verified workers, pay cash/EFT when it's done.
> workitsa.co.za

Ask three or four supportive friends/family to re-share one to *their* Status —
that's real reach into networks you'll never see, from a trusted face.

## Language

These are in plain English (the safe default across mixed groups). For a
predominantly isiZulu / Sesotho / Afrikaans community, a message in that
language lands far warmer — I can produce vetted translations of any of the
member-facing snippets above, but **have a first-language speaker read them
before wide use**: in a trust-based community, clumsy translation costs more
credibility than English does.
