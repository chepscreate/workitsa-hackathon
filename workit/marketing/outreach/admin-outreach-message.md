# Admin outreach messages — WhatsApp / Telegram / Facebook group admins

Drafted 2026-07-06. For the Tier 3/4 targets in
`worker-recruitment-communities.md`. **Draft-and-review: the founder sends
these personally — nothing automated.**

> **See also `whatsapp-community-seeding.md`** — dedicated WhatsApp scripts for
> community groups, churches, and stokvels (added 2026-07-08).

Etiquette (non-negotiable):
- Message the **admin**, never post into a group uninvited.
- One follow-up maximum, 4–6 days later. No reply after that = leave it.
- Personalise the `[bracketed]` parts — admins smell copy-paste.
- If an admin says no, thank them and stop. Their trust with members is the
  asset we're borrowing; never burn it.

---

## 1. First message to a group admin (WhatsApp-length)

> Hi [name] 👋 My name is Tshepo — I'm a South African founder, born and
> raised in Gauteng. I run Workit (workitsa.co.za), a free app where people
> nearby post everyday jobs (gardening, cleaning, plumbing, hair, bakkie
> trips) and verified workers get paid cash or EFT on completion.
>
> I've seen the incredible work you do running [group name] — and the scams
> you have to fight off. That's actually why I built Workit the way I did:
> every worker is ID-verified by a real person, and **nobody ever pays to
> apply**. It's completely free for workers.
>
> Would you be open to sharing it with your members? I have a simple
> one-page flyer + a link I can send you to look over first. No pressure at
> all — and if you'd rather not, I completely understand. Either way, thank
> you for what you do for job seekers. 🙏

**Why this shape:** leads with who we are (a person, not a brand), names
their scam problem before they can suspect us of being one, asks permission,
and gives an easy out.

## 2. Follow-up (once, 4–6 days later)

> Hi [name], just floating this up in case it got buried — no rush at all.
> If it helps: Workit is free for your members (never pay to apply), every
> worker is ID-checked by a real person, and it's built for low-end phones
> and tight data budgets, in English, Afrikaans and isiZulu. Happy to answer
> any questions — or if it's not a fit for [group name], no problem at all.

## 3. Ready-to-post group message (give this TO the admin)

Offer this so the admin doesn't have to write anything — but invite them to
edit it into their own voice; member posts land better in the group's tone.

> 🟢 **New: Workit — free app for finding paid work near you (Gauteng)**
>
> People in your area post everyday jobs — gardening, cleaning, plumbing,
> hair, moving, deliveries, childcare and more. You see the job AND the
> price upfront. Accept it or name your own price. Get paid cash or EFT the
> same day the job is done.
>
> ✅ 100% free — you NEVER pay to apply (anyone asking for money is a scam)
> ✅ Every worker is ID-verified by a real person = employers trust you
> ✅ Works on any phone, light on data, in English / Afrikaans / isiZulu
> ✅ Safety first: share live tracking with family + SOS button on every job
>
> Join free: **workitsa.co.za/signup**

## 4. Telegram channel owners (slightly more formal)

> Hello! I follow [channel] — thank you for the work you put into vetting
> real opportunities. I'm Tshepo, founder of Workit (workitsa.co.za), a free
> South African gig-work platform launching in Gauteng: locals post everyday
> jobs, ID-verified workers accept or counter-offer, and payment is cash/EFT
> on completion. Workers never pay anything. Would you consider a post about
> it? I can send a write-up matching your format, or you're welcome to write
> your own — happy to answer any questions first.

## 5. The referral-code sweetener (optional, for engaged admins)

If an admin is enthusiastic, offer them a way to *see* their impact:

> One more thing — if you sign up yourself, the app gives you a personal
> referral link. Anyone who joins through it is counted for you, and the app
> awards Connector/Ambassador badges as your referrals grow. That way you
> can actually see how many of your members found work through your post.

(Uses the existing in-app referral system — `?ref=` links + referralsCount →
Connector at 3, Ambassador at 10. No money changes hands; keep it that way
until a paid-referral policy is deliberately designed.)
