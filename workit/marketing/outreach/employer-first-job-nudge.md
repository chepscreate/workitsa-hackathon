# Employer first-job nudge — 16 recipients (pulled live 2026-07-15)

**Goal:** convert the employer accounts we already have into first job posts.
Live context: 62 workers vs 1 open job — demand is the bottleneck. These 16
signed up themselves in the last month (mostly the 9–14 Jul campaign wave);
they are the warmest demand leads we have. One employer account (rejected
2026-07-10) is excluded.

**Rules (same as all founder outreach):** personal, founder-sent, one at a
time — never batched, never automated. Send from the founder's WhatsApp.
The links below open WhatsApp with the message pre-typed and personalised —
read it, tweak if you know the person, then hit send.

## Order of operations

1. **Approve the 9 pending employers first** (Admin → Users → role Employer →
   Approve). Employers don't upload docs — pending is just the anti-spam gate,
   and these signed up organically. The pending-variant message says "I've
   just approved your account", so approve each one right before sending.
2. **Send the 7 approved ones any time** — their message says "your account
   is approved and ready".
3. **Follow up by email in ~2 days** for non-responders who left an email
   (template below). Log replies in this file.

## The message (EN — what the links pre-type)

> Hi {name} 👋 Tshepo here, founder of Workit. Your employer account is
> approved and ready! Post your first job at https://workitsa.co.za/post-a-job —
> cleaning, garden, repairs, moving, anything — and local ID-verified workers
> will send you their prices, usually the same day. As an early member I'll
> pin your post to the top of the workers' feed, free. Need something done
> this week? Just reply here and I'll help you set it up myself.

Pending variant swaps the first line for: *"I've just approved your employer
account — you're good to go!"* Afrikaans variant used for the three Afrikaans
names (Logan, Corneluis, Comein) — same content.

Notes on the promises in the message (all true today):
- "ID-verified workers" — workers must pass ID verification before they can work.
- "pin your post to the top, free" — the Boost feature is free during launch;
  boost their job from the job page after they post (or they can tap it themselves).
- "I'll help you set it up" — if they reply with a need, offer to post it for
  them while on the phone; first jobs should be concierge-grade.
