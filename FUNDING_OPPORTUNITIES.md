## ⚡ Act now (deadlines in weeks)

### 0. ⭐ ABSA Youth Entrepreneurship Fund (AYEF) — OPENS AUGUST 2026 · TOP PRIORITY
- **What:** ~$35m fund. **Conditional grants — no repayment, no equity.** Phased
  funding linked to business milestones + mentorship, financial-management and
  governance support, and **market-access / customer-development connections**.
  12-month programme, delivered with the Tshiamo Foundation.
- **Eligibility:** ages **18–35**, ideation through early stage. Detailed criteria
  published when applications open.
- **Fit:** **our strongest match to date.** Grant terms are ideal; our proposal is
  already milestone-structured; "next generation of job creators" is literally
  Workit's model. **The real prize is market access — Absa banks tens of thousands
  of SMEs, which is exactly our missing demand side.**
- **Early enquiries:** absayef@tshiamoimpact.com
- **➡ Full prep: see [`AYEF_READINESS.md`](AYEF_READINESS.md)** — eligibility
  checks, document register, drafted answer bank, and the pre-August action plan.

### 1. Nedbank/Engen Pitch & Polish 2026 — entries OPEN
- **What:** SA's best-known pitch competition (Raizcorp + Nedbank). Winner
  gets ~R1m value: R650k cash (milestone-based) + R350k two-year Raizcorp
  incubation. Even non-winners get pitch training at regional workshops.
- **Fit:** strong — Workit demos well and the story (jobs for the unemployed)
  lands with judges. Zero cost to enter, big exposure.
- **Apply:** https://pitchandpolish.com/

### 2. Jobs Fund — 13th funding round (National Treasury) — closes 30 Jun 2026
- **What:** grants from **R5 million minimum**, this round themed
  *"Catalysing Demand-Led Growth in the Green and Informal Economy"* —
  Workit's exact territory. Open 18 May – 30 June 2026, 15h00.
- **Reality check:** aimed at experienced intermediaries with matched funding
  and audited track records — almost certainly out of reach for an MVP-stage
  company *directly*. Two honest plays: (a) approach an established
  intermediary (incubator, NGO, employment programme like Harambee) about
  partnering with Workit as their tech platform; (b) diarize to apply
  directly in a future round once Workit has traction numbers.
- **Info:** http://www.jobsfund.org.za/ · jobsfund@treasury.gov.za

### 3. Accelerate Africa 2026 — closes 25 Jul 2026
- **What:** pan-African accelerator, up to **$500k investment** for
  early-stage founders ("the Y Combinator of Africa", run by Iyin Aboyeji).
- **Fit:** medium — competitive and equity-based, but a marketplace tackling
  SA unemployment is a credible applicant. Free to apply.

## 🎯 Best structural fits (apply year-round)

### 4. NYDA Grant Programme — top priority
- **What:** **non-repayable grants R1,000–R250,000** for youth (18–35),
  with tech businesses qualifying at the top band. Free Business Management
  Training (required before applying), mentorship, market linkages.
- **Eligibility:** SA citizen, 18–35, business 100% youth-owned, operating in
  SA, annual turnover under R750k, at least one member working in the
  business full-time.
- **How:** complete the free BMT course, then apply via the NYDA portal or a
  branch. Applications open year-round.
- **Fit:** excellent — this is exactly what it exists for. The ask: working
  capital for go-to-market (the gap named in GO_TO_MARKET.md), verification
  ops, and data/hosting costs.
- **Apply:** https://www.nyda.gov.za/

### 5. Youth Challenge Fund (DSBD via SEFA/SEDFA)
- **What:** youth start-up programme explicitly aimed at **digital-economy
  youth businesses** — funding plus business development support and
  mentorship.
- **Eligibility:** 18–35, CIPC-registered, 100% SA-owned, founder full-time
  in the business, commercially viable idea.
- **How:** register on https://sefa.finfind.co.za or email YCF@sefa.org.za ·
  helpline 012 748 9600.
- **Fit:** excellent — "youth + digital + job creation" is the whole brief.

### 6. SEFA/SEDFA direct funding
- **What:** loans and blended products R50k–R15m for registered SMMEs (SEFA,
  SEDA and the Co-op Bank agency merged into **SEDFA**). 2026 youth-aimed
  packages reported around R350k (R300k asset finance + R50k grant portion).
- **Fit:** good once Workit has revenue (loans need repayment ability) —
  file under "after monetization starts", except the grant-portion youth
  products, which are worth a conversation now.
- **Apply:** https://sefa.finfind.co.za

## 🚀 Tech / innovation route (Gauteng advantage)

### 7. The Innovation Hub (Gauteng) — Maxum incubator + GAP competition
- **What:** Gauteng's official innovation agency. **Maxum Smart Business
  Incubator** takes ICT startups (business support, mentorship, subsidised
  space, investor access); the annual **Gauteng Accelerator Programme (GAP)
  Innovation Competition** awards prize funding + incubation (2025/26
  finalists just announced — watch for the next intake announcement,
  typically mid-year).
- **Fit:** excellent and local — Workit is a Gauteng ICT platform creating
  jobs, which is GGDA's mandate in one sentence.
- **Apply:** https://www.theinnovationhub.com/ (Business Incubation
  Application Form on the site)

### 8. mLab Southern Africa
- **What:** mobile/tech incubator hosted at the Innovation Hub — developer
  support, product help, startup programmes. Free/sponsored.
- **Fit:** good — natural home for a mobile-first PWA; also a credibility
  badge for other funders.
- **Apply:** https://mlab.co.za/

### 9. Technology Innovation Agency (TIA) Seed Fund
- **What:** seed grants (typically up to ~R1m) to de-risk technology toward
  commercialisation — **accessed via implementing partners** (university
  TTOs and incubators, e.g. the TuksNovation × TIA Seed Fund Innovation
  Challenge). TIA also co-funds new private vehicles:
- **Aions Seed Fund I** — launched June 2026, R100m (TIA committed R40m),
  backing SA digital-economy startups — equity investment, worth a pitch.
- **High Impact Seed Fund of Funds** — R300m (SA SME Fund + TIA + E Squared)
  flowing to startups through portfolio funds.
- **Fit:** good — route in through an incubator (Maxum/mLab/TuksNovation
  makes this easier, another reason to do #7/#8 first).
- **Info:** https://www.tia.org.za/funding-instruments/

## 🏆 Social-impact funders (Workit's strongest narrative)

### 10. SAB Foundation — Social Innovation Awards (next round ~Feb 2027)
- **What:** grants **R300k–R1.3m** + mentorship for innovations serving
  youth, women, rural communities and people with disabilities. 2026 round
  closed 16 March; applications open each February.
- **Fit:** excellent — verified gig work for unemployed youth is a textbook
  entry. **Diarize for February 2027** and build the impact numbers now
  (jobs completed, worker earnings — the GA4 funnel + stats page provide
  this evidence).
- **Info:** https://sabfoundation.co.za/social-innovation-awards

### 11. SAB Foundation — Tholoana Enterprise Programme (opens ~Aug/Sep)
- **What:** 18-month enterprise development programme: skills, mentorship,
  market access and grant funding. ~60 businesses/year; prioritises youth-,
  women- and disability-led businesses.
- **Eligibility:** trading ≥ 12 months, ≥51% black-owned and managed,
  founder full-time, SA-registered. Last round's deadline was 8 September —
  watch from August.
- **Apply:** https://sabfoundation.co.za/tholoana-enterprise-programme

### 12. Google for Startups — Black Founders Fund Africa (next cohort ~2027)
- **What:** **equity-free** cash up to **$150,000** + $200k Google Cloud
  credits + mentorship. 2026 applications closed in March.
- **Fit:** excellent — Black-founded, Africa-based tech startup is the exact
  profile; the cloud credits alone would cover Workit's Firebase bill for
  years. **Diarize for early 2027.**
- **Info:** https://startup.google.com/programs/black-founders-fund/africa/

- **Fit:** strong *later* — when Workit raises for provincial expansion, a
